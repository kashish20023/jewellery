Client Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding our BIS Hallmarking, certified natural diamonds, armored shipping, and custom commissions.

Every single gold creation is hallmarked by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and carries the official 6-digit alphanumeric HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) laser-engraved alongside the purity stamp (916 for 22 Karat, 750 for 18 Karat). You can verify your piece instantly using the official BIS Care mobile application. Natural solitaires and diamond suites are additionally certified by independent laboratories like IGI or SGL.

We provide complimentary, 100% insured door-to-door transit across all serviceable PIN codes in India via specialized secure logistics partners (such as Sequel Logistics and BVC Logistics). Every shipment is transported in an armored tamper-evident security bag and requires a secure OTP verification and government-issued photo ID signature upon delivery.

In strict accordance with Government of India regulations (Rule 114B of the Income Tax Act), furnishing a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) is legally required for any single precious jewellery transaction equal to or exceeding ₹2,00,000 (INR). Our secure checkout will seamlessly prompt for PAN details when applicable.

Jadau Polki jewellery should always be preserved in airtight containers or velvet-lined boxes away from moisture, humidity, and direct chemical sprays like perfumes or hairsprays. Never submerge Jadau Polki pieces in water or ultrasonic cleaners, as moisture can alter the delicate 24K gold foil (Daak). We recommend wiping your jewellery with a clean, soft suede cloth after every wear.
